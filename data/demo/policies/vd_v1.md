# 1 Scope
This policy applies to every third party that will receive, store or process Kestrel Mutual customer data.

# 2 Vendor tiers

## 2.1 Tiering
Vendor management assigns each vendor a tier when it is added to the vendor register. Tier 1 vendors receive customer data or support a critical service. Tier 2 vendors do neither.

# 3 Vendor review

## 3.1 Review before data access
A vendor must complete Kestrel Mutual’s vendor review, and hold Approved status in the vendor register, before receiving customer data.

## 3.2 Review content
Vendor review covers security controls, data location and subprocessors. A completed review is valid for twelve months from its approval date.

## 3.3 Expired reviews
A vendor whose review has expired is treated as unreviewed until a new review is approved.

## 3.4 Conditional approval exception
Vendor management may grant Conditional status for up to 90 days while agreed remediation actions are completed. A vendor with Conditional status may receive customer data only if the Chief Information Security Officer approves this in writing, instead of the Approved status that clause 3.1 requires.

# 4 Contracts

## 4.1 Data processing terms
A vendor that processes customer data must sign Kestrel Mutual’s data processing terms before the first transfer of customer data.

## 4.2 Subprocessors
A vendor must notify Kestrel Mutual at least 30 days before adding a subprocessor that will handle customer data.
