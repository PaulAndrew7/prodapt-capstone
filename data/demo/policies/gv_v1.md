# 1 Purpose
This standard sets how Kestrel Mutual policies are owned, dated, applied and excepted.

# 2 Ownership

## 2.1 Policy owners
Each policy has a named owner who keeps it current and reviews it at least every two years.

# 3 Effective dates

## 3.1 Which version applies
An activity is assessed against the policy versions in effect on the date the activity takes place.

# 4 Conflicts and exceptions

## 4.1 Conflicting policies
Where two policies set conflicting requirements for the same activity and neither states which prevails, the conflict must be escalated to the Policy Committee. The activity must not proceed until the committee records a decision.

## 4.2 Policy exceptions
An exception to a policy requires written approval from the policy owner, must state an expiry date no later than 12 months after approval, and must be recorded in the exceptions register.
