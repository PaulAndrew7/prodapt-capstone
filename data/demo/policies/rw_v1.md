# 1 Purpose
This policy sets when and how Kestrel Mutual staff may work away from the office.

# 2 Eligibility

## 2.1 Eligible roles
Staff may work remotely for up to three days a week where their line manager agrees the role is suitable.

# 3 Equipment and security

## 3.1 Company devices
Remote work must be done on a Kestrel Mutual managed device. Personal devices must not be used to access customer data.

## 3.2 Networks
Staff connect to Kestrel Mutual systems through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN connected.

## 3.3 Printing
Customer data must not be printed at home.

# 4 Working abroad

## 4.1 Working from another country
Working remotely from outside the United Kingdom for more than 10 working days in a calendar year requires approval from the Head of People.
