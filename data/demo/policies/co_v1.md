# 1 Purpose
This policy sets the checks Kestrel Mutual completes before it accepts a new customer.

# 2 Identity verification

## 2.1 Verification before activation
A customer’s identity must be verified before their policy is activated.

## 2.2 Acceptable evidence
Identity is verified by an electronic check against two independent sources, or by a certified copy of a passport or driving licence.

## 2.3 Incomplete verification
If verification is still incomplete 30 days after the application, the application is closed and any payment is refunded.

# 3 Enhanced due diligence

## 3.1 Triggers
Enhanced due diligence is required when the annual premium exceeds £50,000, when the customer is a politically exposed person, or when the premium is paid by a third party.

## 3.2 Approval
A customer who requires enhanced due diligence may be onboarded only with approval from the Money Laundering Reporting Officer.

# 4 Exceptions

## 4.1 Existing customers exception
Existing customers whose identity was verified within the last three years do not need to repeat identity verification under clause 2.1 when they buy another product.
