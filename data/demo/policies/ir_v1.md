# 1 Purpose
This policy sets how Kestrel Mutual reports, handles and learns from security incidents.

# 2 Definitions

## 2.1 Security incident
A “security incident” is any event that compromises, or could compromise, the confidentiality, integrity or availability of Kestrel Mutual information.

## 2.2 Personal data breach
A “personal data breach” is a security incident that leads to the accidental or unlawful loss, alteration or disclosure of, or access to, customer personal data.

# 3 Reporting

## 3.1 Staff reporting
Any member of staff who suspects a security incident must report it to the Security Operations Centre within one hour of noticing it.

## 3.2 No investigation before reporting
Staff must not investigate a suspected incident themselves before reporting it.

# 4 Handling

## 4.1 Severity
The Security Operations Centre assigns each incident a severity from 1 (critical) to 4 (low) within four hours of the report.

## 4.2 Data Protection Officer notification
If a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to customers, the Data Protection Officer must be told within 24 hours of the breach being confirmed.

## 4.3 Customer communication
Customers are contacted about an incident only through messages approved by the Head of Communications.

# 5 Review

## 5.1 Post-incident review
A post-incident review is completed within 20 working days of closing any severity 1 or severity 2 incident.
