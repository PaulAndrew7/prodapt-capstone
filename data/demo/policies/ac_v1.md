# 1 Purpose
This policy sets how people are given, and lose, access to Kestrel Mutual systems that hold customer data.

# 2 Production access

## 2.1 Least privilege
Access to production systems holding customer data is granted at the lowest level that allows the person to do their assigned work.

## 2.2 Named accounts
Every person uses a named account. Shared accounts must not be used on production systems.

## 2.3 Standing access
Standing production access requires approval from the system owner and is reviewed under clause 3.1.

## 2.4 Temporary access
Temporary production access requires approval from the requester’s line manager and expires automatically after 14 days.

## 2.5 Contractors
Contractors may hold temporary production access only while a signed confidentiality agreement is on file.

# 3 Access reviews

## 3.1 Quarterly review
System owners review all production access every quarter and remove access that is no longer needed within five working days of the review.

## 3.2 Leavers
Access for a person leaving Kestrel Mutual is removed by the end of their last working day.
