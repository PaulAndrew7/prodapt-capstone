# 1 Purpose
This policy sets the conditions under which Kestrel Mutual may share customer data with any party outside the organization.

# 2 Scope

## 2.1 Covered data
This policy covers customer data in any form, including extracts, reports and pseudonymised datasets from which a customer could be re-identified.

## 2.2 Internal transfers
Transfers between Kestrel Mutual teams are not external sharing. The Access Control Policy governs them.

# 3 Roles

## 3.1 Data owner
Each customer dataset has a named data owner at Head of Department level or above. The data owner is accountable for decisions about external sharing of that dataset.

# 4 External sharing

## 4.1 Definition of external sharing
“External sharing” means any transfer of, or remote access to, customer data by a party outside Kestrel Mutual, including vendors and affiliates operating as a separate legal entity.

## 4.2 Data-owner approval
External sharing of customer data requires written approval from the data owner, recorded in the data-sharing register before any transfer takes place.

## 4.3 Purpose limitation
The fields shared must be limited to those necessary for the documented purpose of the sharing. The purpose and the field list must be recorded with the approval.

## 4.4 Legal disclosure exception
Transfers required by law or by a regulator’s written request follow the Legal Disclosure Procedure instead of clause 4.2. Legal is the approver for these transfers.

# 5 Register

## 5.1 Register upkeep
The data owner reviews each open entry in the data-sharing register every twelve months and closes entries for sharing that has ended.

## 5.2 Sharing without approval
Any external sharing that took place without the approval required by clause 4.2 must be reported as a security incident under the Incident Response Policy within one working day of discovery.
