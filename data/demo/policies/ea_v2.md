# 1 Purpose
This policy sets who approves business expenses and what evidence a claim needs.

# 2 Approval thresholds

## 2.1 Line manager approval
Expenses up to £500 per claim require approval from the claimant’s line manager.

## 2.2 Budget holder approval
Expenses above £500 and up to £10,000 per claim require approval from the budget holder in addition to the line manager.

## 2.3 Financial Controller approval
Expenses above £10,000 per claim require approval from the Financial Controller.

## 2.4 No splitting
A single purchase must not be split into several claims to stay below an approval threshold.

# 3 Evidence

## 3.1 Receipts
Every claim above £25 must include an itemised receipt.

## 3.2 Submission deadline
Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred.

## 3.3 Hospitality for public officials
Any gift or hospitality offered to a public official requires prior written approval from the Compliance team, whatever its value.

# 4 Currency

## 4.1 Foreign currency
Claims in another currency are converted to pounds sterling at the exchange rate on the date of the expense. The thresholds in this policy apply to the converted amount.
