# 1 Purpose
This policy sets how long Kestrel Mutual keeps customer records and how they are deleted.

# 2 Definitions

## 2.1 Record
A “record” is any information created or received in the course of business that documents a decision, transaction or communication, in any format.

# 3 Responsibilities

## 3.1 Record owners
Each record type has a record owner who makes sure records are kept for the period in this policy and no longer.

# 4 Customer records

## 4.1 Customer account records
Customer account records are retained for six years after the customer relationship ends, then deleted.

## 4.2 Marketing preferences
Marketing consent records are retained while the customer relationship lasts and for two years afterwards.

# 5 Claim records

## 5.1 Standard retention
Customer claim records are retained for seven years after the claim closes, then deleted.

## 5.2 Copies and derived datasets
Copies of claim records, including extracts and datasets derived from them, follow the same retention period as the source records.

## 5.3 Deletion holds
Records under a litigation or regulatory hold must not be deleted until Legal releases the hold, regardless of any deletion request.

# 6 Deletion

## 6.1 Deletion method
Deletion must remove a record from live systems within 30 days of the end of its retention period and from backups within a further 90 days.
